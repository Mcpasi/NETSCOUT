NetScout searches the web, GitHub, Hacker News, forums, Reddit and YouTube from a native Android app. All six sources work without your own API keys. You can optionally configure existing credentials or a custom SearXNG instance. The user interface is in German.

• Keywords and exact phrases, optional date ranges and source selection
• Combined results with original links and per-source status
• On-device relevance ranking with a bundled multilingual model
• Sort by relevance or known publication dates
• Save up to 20 favorites locally, read them offline and remove them
• No ads, telemetry or NetScout account

Search terms are sent to the selected services or their public search index. Several services are proprietary; availability, index coverage and service limits affect searches. Reddit and YouTube use index search unless you supply your own credentials. A custom SearXNG service can be configured.

The local model needs no download after installation and no remote AI service. It increases APK size and uses additional processing time. Its judgments can be wrong. Results without a known publication date are excluded when a date filter is active.

Favorites stay in private app storage without cloud synchronization. Optional credentials are encrypted using Android Keystore. Requires Android 8.0 or newer.
